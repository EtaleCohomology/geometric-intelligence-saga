# Side Stories

Étale Cohomology publishes on GitHub the theory documents of Geometric Intelligence (GI) theory (several files) and GI case studies (by industry and by policy domain). Related articles are also published on Qiita.

The Qiita articles include introductions to GI theory, as well as a series of short stories in which the characters of the case studies propose and debate ideas while GI theory is being built.

This folder publishes, on GitHub, five of those articles that were released on Qiita as side stories (Side Stories 1–5, published in late March 2026), together with one Qiita article that was not released as a side story but can be regarded as the sixth one (Side Story 6).
