# Side Story 1: "Design seven different AI agents for seven companies — no duplicates" — Designing AI agents on a business-environment manifold digital twin (Part 1)

Riem's venture receives requests from seven world-leading companies to validate the accuracy of AI agents. There are two conditions: propose a different agent architecture for each of the seven companies, with no duplicates; and propose two agents for each company — one without a body, and one that moves a body inside the digital twin (embodied AI). Professor Liluru and the members of the lab organise the landscape of AI agent architectures and assign agents to six of the companies along three axes: time scale, structure of uncertainty, and granularity of decisions.
